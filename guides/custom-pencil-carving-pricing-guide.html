2. Step-by-Step Commission Workflow

  1. Direct Consultation: Message Harsh on WhatsApp (+91 98706 20748) or call directly with your concept, name to carve, or temple reference.
  2. Blueprint & Scale Estimation: Harsh provides a sketch or digital scale preview showing letter proportions on the 0.5mm to 2.0mm lead core.
  3. Sculpting & Progress Updates: You receive microscopic high-resolution video and photo updates directly from the Rishikesh studio as the carving takes shape.
  4. Final Approval & Preservation: The finished piece is photographed against a millimetre ruler, encased in the hermetic bell jar, and sealed with the studio wax seal.
  5. Insured Express Delivery: Shipped with full transit insurance and live tracking directly to your doorstep.
