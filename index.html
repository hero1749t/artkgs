The Artist & The River

"The silence of the Ganga ghats, Hindi poetry, and the contradictions of modern life shape my work."

Harsh is a self-taught visual artist practicing under the brand Artkgs on the holy banks of river Ganga in Rishikesh, Uttarakhand. His practice explores the profound philosophical concept of "Incompleteness as Wholeness" (अपूर्णता में ही पूर्णता).

Sitting on the river ghats with magnifying lenses and fine surgical needles, Harsh sculpts intricate temples and suspension bridges onto brittle graphite pencil leads, while painting the eternal sacred majesty of Kedarnath and Devbhoomi on canvas.

His signature series Sugarasur satirizes modern society's dependencies, while his literary blog "Kavitao ka Canvas" unites poetry with visual art.

Knowledge Base • FAQs

Questions About Pencil Art & Commissions

Everything you need to know about Harsh's micro graphite sculptures, preservation, bespoke gifting, and Rishikesh studio orders.

What is miniature pencil lead carving and how is it created?
Miniature pencil lead carving is an extraordinarily delicate fine art where architectural wonders, instruments, deities, and letters are hand-chiseled directly onto the graphite lead of standard graphite pencils (ranging from 0.5mm to 2.0mm). Harsh employs precision surgical blades, jeweler needles, and stereoscopic optical magnification while controlling his heartbeat and breathing to carve without snapping the graphite.
How fragile is a carved pencil tip, and how long does it last?
Graphite itself is inert and does not rust, oxidize, or degrade over time. However, due to its microscopic slender structure, each finished pencil sculpture is permanently mounted inside a sealed, impact-resistant acrylic display capsule with a crystal-clear magnifying dome, or inside a museum-grade velvet casing. Handled properly inside its case, it lasts for generations.
Can I order custom names, wedding anniversary gifts, or temple carvings?
Absolutely. Bespoke commissions are a core specialty of ArtKGS. You can request loved ones' names carved letter-by-letter on a single pencil lead, micro interlocking hearts, sacred temple architecture (Ram Mandir, Kedarnath, Somnath), or divine deities. Each bespoke order includes customized wooden or glass packaging suitable for luxury gifting.
